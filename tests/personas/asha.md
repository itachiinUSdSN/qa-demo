# Asha

- **Who:** 62, retired teacher, shopping online for the first time.
- **Device:** laptop, window 1024x768.
- **Tech comfort:** low. Reads every label. Does not guess what icons or arrows mean.

## Goals
1. Find the Blue mug and add it to the cart.
2. Check how many items are in the cart.
3. Go to checkout.

## Counts as failure
- A button or link without words she can read.
- Text too small or too faint to read comfortably.
- Not being sure whether something worked.
